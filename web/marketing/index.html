Questions

Tattoo marketplace FAQ

What is TattsBid?

TattsBid is a marketplace that connects customers with tattoo artists. You post a request; artists bid. TattsBid does not provide tattoo services and is not a party to the work itself.

How do I find a tattoo artist for a custom piece?

Upload a photo, write the brief, and set a starting bid. Artists who can take the job send bids. Compare price, style, and availability, then select a winner.

Can tattoo artists advertise finished work?

Yes. Artists can post portfolio tattoos to Tattsagram, the in-app feed, so customers can see real healed or fresh work before they bid or book.

Is TattsBid a tattoo studio?

No. Each artist works independently. Confirm the studio, licensing, and aftercare with the person you hire.
